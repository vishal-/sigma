# Tutorog

**Discover. Learn. Grow.**

Tutorog is a community-driven platform that helps learners discover the best tutors, coaches, academies, and mentors across academics, sports, arts, hobbies, music, languages, technology, and much more.

Whether someone is looking for a nearby mathematics tutor, a dance academy, a cricket coach, a robotics class, or a music teacher, Tutorog aims to make finding quality learning opportunities simple, transparent, and accessible.

---

# Our Vision

Learning happens everywhere—not just in schools and colleges.

Thousands of passionate tutors, coaches, artists, and mentors teach valuable skills every day, yet many struggle to build an online presence or reach the right students.

Tutorog exists to bridge that gap by creating a trusted platform where learners can discover opportunities and educators can grow their communities.

Our long-term vision is to become the home of lifelong learning, connecting students with educators across every discipline and stage of life.

---

# What Tutorog Offers

### For Learners

* Discover nearby and online learning opportunities
* Explore tutors and academies across multiple categories
* Compare courses, pricing, reviews, and facilities
* Find the right learning environment based on individual goals
* Connect directly with educators

### For Tutors & Academies

* Create a professional public profile
* Showcase courses, facilities, and achievements
* Improve online visibility
* Reach more students organically
* Build trust through reviews and verified information

---

# Community First

Tutorog is being built with a community-first approach.

Our initial focus is helping educators establish their online presence while making it easier for students and parents to discover quality learning experiences.

The goal is to build a thriving ecosystem before introducing premium business tools.

---

# Long-Term Vision

As the platform grows, Tutorog will evolve into a complete operating platform for educational organizations.

Future capabilities may include:

* Student admissions
* Course management
* Batch management
* Attendance tracking
* Fee management
* Learning materials
* Assessments
* Progress tracking
* Parent communication
* Messaging & notifications
* Analytics & reporting
* Online enrollments
* Payments
* Reviews & reputation management
* Business insights

These features will be introduced gradually based on community feedback and real-world needs.

# Core Principles

### Community Driven

Every feature should strengthen the connection between learners and educators.

### Simple First

Build the simplest solution that solves a real problem before expanding functionality.

### Discoverability

Help every educator—whether an individual tutor or a large academy—be easily found by the right students.

### Trust

Promote transparency through verified profiles, authentic reviews, and accurate information.

### Accessibility

Support learning across all ages, interests, locations, and skill levels.
